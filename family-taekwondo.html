How Family Taekwondo Runs

Not Two Classes Under One Roof. One Class.

Physical

Technique Taught to One Standard

Kyle Anderson's patterns expertise and Dafydd Sanders' competitive record set the technical bar — for every belt in the room, adult or child. Pace is matched to the person. The standard isn't.

Mental

Tested Side by Side, Not Separately

A parent working the same pattern their child is working, under the same correction from the same coach, changes what the correction means to both of them. Nobody's just watching from the sideline.

Identity

What the Family Becomes Together

The pattern is the vehicle. What a family walks out with — a shared standard, a shared Tuesday and Thursday, a shared thing they're both working toward — is the actual outcome Family Taekwondo is built to produce.

Nothing Is Given Away

Every Grade Is Earned — By Every Belt in the House.

Family Taekwondo runs on the same graded syllabus as every Eagle DS programme — belt by belt, skill by skill, checked in front of a coach who actually checked. A parent's blue belt and a child's blue belt were earned against the identical standard. Kyle Anderson's patterns background means the syllabus is taught by someone who has been tested on it at World Cup level himself — not someone teaching a form he's only ever demonstrated.

Before You Book

Questions Every Family Actually Asks.

Do we need any experience — any of us?

No. Most families start with none. The syllabus is structured so a complete beginner and a family member who's trained before are both working at the right level from day one.

Who's actually coaching the class?

Dafydd Sanders and Kyle Anderson lead Family Taekwondo. Dafydd brings a World #10 taekwondo ranking across multiple Olympic campaigns, plus WAKO Worlds kickboxing and K1, IMMAF Worlds MMA, and an All Japan karate title. Kyle is the 2013 World Cup Champion (Argentina) in taekwondo sparring, a patterns specialist, and a 2nd degree blackbelt in both ITF and WT taekwondo — certified across both federations.

When does Family Taekwondo train?

Tuesdays and Thursdays, 6:00pm. Two sessions a week — enough to build a real habit without overcommitting a whole family's week.

Do parents and kids train in the same session?

Yes — that's the point of Family Taekwondo. Everyone works the same pattern and the same standard, at a pace matched to what each person has demonstrated, not their age.

Is it safe for younger kids and older parents in the same room?

Technique comes before contact, every time. Sparring is introduced progressively and only once control has been shown — closely supervised throughout. Eagle DS holds a zero-concussion record across 900+ matches.

Does our family have to compete?

No. Competing through the Combat Games Series is one path, not a requirement. Plenty of families train for fitness, shared time and skill and never step into a competitive bout.

What's expected from us every session?

Not perfection. What's expected, every time, from every belt in the family:

  • Effort
  • Respect
  • Accountability
  • Coachability

What should we bring to our first session?

Comfortable sports clothing and a drink bottle — for everyone training. Equipment for the first session is provided. If you continue, you'll be advised exactly what's needed as each family member progresses.
